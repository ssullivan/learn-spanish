# Spanish Learning Games

Static HTML Spanish lessons and games served with GitHub Pages at https://ssullivan.github.io/learn-spanish/ (from `main`, repo root). No build step for the site itself; `tools/` scripts generate assets and check the site. The structure follows its sister site, `~/learn-cantonese`: when a feature exists there (Say It Back, Reply Match, word tiles, the audio review page), port it from there rather than inventing a new design, adapting it to Spanish.

## Code standards
All code must be **readable, maintainable and DRY, with descriptive names**.
- Anything two pages or units share belongs in `shared/` (browser) or `tools/` (Node), not in copies. Generated files are never edited by hand; edit their source and regenerate.
- Derive, don't type: syllables and stress come from `shared/spanish.js`; future verb forms, numbers and plurals get a shared module with tests in the same way.
- Descriptive names (`soundAlikes`, `stressedSyllable`, `shouldRetryAzure`), not `e`, `gap` or `tries`. When porting code from learn-cantonese, rename as you port.
- Each shared file opens with a header comment documenting its API. Keep it up to date.

Each `unit<N>/CLAUDE.md` holds that unit's specifics: its topic and grammar point, games, audio and drawing quirks, and what later units use from it. Read it before working in a unit, and keep it up to date when the unit changes.

## Words, audio and pictures
- Every word lives once, in the dictionary `words/words.js`, under the unit that teaches it (`Words.add(n, [...])`, which sets `taught: n`): `{ id, spanish, english, note?, img?, say?, ssml?, voice? }`. Its audio, picture and drawing are in `words/`. Every field is listed with the kind of value it holds in `tools/vocab-fields.mjs`, and check.mjs fails on any other field, a wrong value, or an id that names no entry: add a new field there first. `img: false` means no picture.
- A word's id is its plain spelling (`Spanish.plainId`: lower case, no accents, ñ as `ny`, words joined by `-`: `piña` → `pinya`, `¡Buenos días!` → `buenos-dias`). Ids are unique on the site: when two words would share one, the one with the accent mark gets `-tilde` (`papa`, `papa-tilde`), or another gets a suffix naming its sense. check.mjs enforces both, and that no two homes define the same spelling with the same voice.
- A unit's `vocab.js` lists the words it uses with `Words.get(id)` or `...Words.list('a b c')`, in the groups its pages read, adding its own fields to the copy: `{ ...Words.get('perro'), letter: 'rr' }`. It uses only words taught in it or earlier, and lists every word it teaches; check.mjs enforces both. A new word goes in the dictionary under the unit that first teaches it; never write one into a `vocab.js`.
- `Units.word(n, id)` borrows unit n's own version of an entry (a phrase it built, or a word with what unit n added). Its files stay where they are. Pages load the `vocab.js` of the units their unit borrows from this way, and no others (`tools/site.mjs` borrowsFrom).
- Spelling: Mexican Spanish (carro, computadora, jugo, ustedes; no vosotros). `spanish` is typed in composed Unicode with real accent marks, questions open with `¿` and exclamations with `¡`; check.mjs enforces all three.
- Pictures: a word's drawing goes in `words/art.mjs`, in the section of the unit that teaches it (a function, so its helpers stay its own); a phrase's in its unit's `art.mjs`. Run `node tools/draw.mjs` (which also deletes svgs no longer drawn). Reuse or extend `tools/svg.mjs` parts instead of copying markup. 128×128 flat style, fixed colours, transparent background, a `<title>`. Render and look at new drawings before committing (headless Chrome: `google-chrome --headless=new --screenshot=out.png file://...`).

### Audio
- `node tools/tts.mjs [words|unitN ...] [--only id,id] [--force]` regenerates only entries whose text or voice changed, saving `manifest.json` after every clip. Without `--only` it also moves a renamed entry's clip to its new id, or from a unit to `words/` when the word moves to the dictionary, and deletes clips of entries that are gone, so renaming costs no new audio.
- The voice is Azure's `es-MX-DaliaNeural` (`Words.voice` and each unit's `voice`); `es-MX-JorgeNeural` is the male voice for a second speaker. `say` speaks other text; `ssml` overrides the SSML inside `<voice>` for a word the voice misreads.
- Credentials come from `~/.config/learning-spanish/config.env` (`AZURE_SPEECH_KEY`, `AZURE_SPEECH_REGION`), read by `tools/site.mjs`. **Never put a key in any committed file** (code, docs, fixtures, logs, commit messages) and never print one; check.mjs fails if any file holds one.
- Clips are not machine-checked yet: audio-lang-tools (`~/audio-lang-tools`, used by learn-cantonese) scores Cantonese tones. Listen to new clips, especially sound-alike pairs (pero/perro, papa/papá), and tell the user which ones you could not verify.

## Pages
- Load `shared/` with `../shared/...`; relative links only (the site is served under `/learn-spanish/`).
- Every page links the favicon right after its viewport meta: `<link rel="icon" href="../favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">` (`favicon.svg` on the root page).
- Local CSS and JS carry a `?v=<hash>` stamp written by `node tools/check.mjs --fix`; never edit one by hand.
- Templates: `unit1/learn.html` + `lessons.js` for learn pages, `unit1/match.*` for games. Reusable UI goes in `shared/`, not a unit's script.
- Script order: `core.js`, `audio.js` (+ `spanish.js` if used), then `learn.js` or `game.js` (+ `stress.js`...), then the markers `<!-- vocab: written by node tools/check.mjs --fix -->` … `<!-- /vocab -->`, then the page script. `--fix` writes the vocab scripts between the markers; never write them by hand.
- Show Spanish through `Core.es(text)` (gives it `lang="es-MX"`), never hand-built spans in scripts.
- No emoji in the UI; use text, ▶, ★.
- Game and learn pages link back with `href="./"` ("← Unit N"); unit pages with `href="../"` ("← All units").
- A new learn page or game gets a card on `unit<N>/index.html`.
- The root `index.html` lists every unit by stage (`h2.stage-head`); units not built are `div.card.soon` placeholders. A new unit: create `unit<N>/` like `unit1/` (with its CLAUDE.md), turn its placeholder into an `a.card` with a picture, and keep the eyebrow's unit count right.
- `localStorage` is shared site-wide: keys `u<N>-learn`, `u<N>-<game>`.
- `.nojekyll` serves files as-is. The site is all rights reserved (`LICENSE`); the copyright line comes from `.page::after` in `theme.css`, so no footers.
- Avoid gendered pronouns for named people in lessons; reword instead.

## Checking a change
1. `node tools/check.mjs --fix` must print "All checks passed". Tests must be able to fail: after writing one, break the code on purpose and confirm it's reported. The same checks run before every commit and on every push; never skip the hook with `--no-verify`.
2. Serve locally (`python3 -m http.server`) and play the affected pages through in a browser at 390px and desktop, light and dark: no horizontal scroll, all audio plays, quizzes and levels complete, timers, stars and unlocks work.

## Roadmap
Units build on each other: each reuses earlier words and adds one grammar point. Units 1–2 are built. **The order of units 3 onward is provisional** and may change until each is built; once a unit is built, its number is final.

| Stage | Units |
|---|---|
| Foundations | 1 Sounds & Spelling *Sonidos* · 2 Greetings *Saludos* · 3 Me & You *Yo y tú* · 4 Numbers *Números* · 5 Things & Articles *Cosas* · 6 Money & Shopping *De compras* |
| Daily life | 7 At the Taquería · 8 At the Café · 9 Time & Dates *La hora* · 10 Family *La familia* · 11 Getting Around *¿Dónde está?* · 12 Colours & Clothes *La ropa* · 13 Weather *El clima* · 14 Body & Health *El cuerpo* |
| Conversation | 15 Daily Routine *Mi día* · 16 Likes & Hobbies *Me gusta* · 17 Feelings · 18 Comparing · 19 Past Weekend · 20 Plans *Planes* |

Planned site-wide games, besides Stress Detective and Reply Match: **Say It Back** (record yourself and compare pitch with the model; port `shared/pitch.js` and `sayit.*` from learn-cantonese) and **Dictado** (hear a word or sentence and type it, accents included). Verb units (15 on) get a shared, tested `conjugate.js`.
